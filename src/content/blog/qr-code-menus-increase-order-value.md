For decades, the restaurant industry relied on physical menus and human servers to drive sales. But data from the past three years shows a fascinating trend: restaurants that switch to interactive QR code menus see an average order value (AOV) increase of 20% to 25%.

Why does this happen?

### 1. Zero "Wait Time" Friction
When a customer decides they want a second beer or an extra side of fries, they have a short window of intent. If they have to wait 5 minutes to catch a server's eye, they often change their mind. With a QR menu, that order is sent to the kitchen in 10 seconds. Frictionless ordering directly correlates to higher volume.

### 2. Perfect Upselling, Every Time
Servers get busy. They forget to ask, "Would you like to add avocado for $2?" or "Would you like to make that a large?" A digital ordering system never forgets. It systematically presents high-margin add-ons right at the moment of decision, driving up the ticket size effortlessly.

### 3. The "Visual" Appetite
A printed text menu relies on the customer's imagination. A digital menu with high-resolution, appetizing photos triggers a biological response. When customers *see* a perfectly glazed dessert or a frosty cocktail, their likelihood to purchase skyrockets.

### 4. Reduced Social Pressure
Psychological studies show that customers sometimes feel judged or rushed when ordering from a server. Ordering via a phone provides a private, unpressured environment where customers feel comfortable ordering exactly what they want—which often means ordering more.

By implementing NoMenu's dynamic QR ordering, restaurants aren't just saving on labor; they are actively driving top-line revenue growth.
