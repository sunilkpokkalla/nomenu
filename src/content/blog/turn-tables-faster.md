Friday night at 7:30 PM. The lobby is packed, wait times are creeping up to 45 minutes, and your guests are getting impatient. Every restaurant manager knows this feeling. Turning tables efficiently during peak rush is the single biggest factor in weekend profitability.

Here are strategies to increase table turnover without sacrificing the guest experience.

### 1. The 3-Minute Greeter Rule
The clock starts the moment a guest sits down. Ensure a staff member greets the table within 3 minutes to drop water and menus. If you are using a digital QR system, this step is handled automatically—guests sit, scan, and start browsing immediately while the host is still walking away.

### 2. Streamline the Ordering Process
Taking an order for a 6-top can take a server up to 10 minutes (answering questions, running back to the kitchen to check on allergies, punching it into the POS). Digital ordering eliminates this entirely. The kitchen receives the order perfectly formatted, and the server can focus on running food and clearing tables.

### 3. The Check-Out Bottleneck
This is where restaurants lose the most time. 
1. Guest asks for the check. (2 mins)
2. Server prints the check and drops it off. (3 mins)
3. Guest puts a card down. (2 mins)
4. Server runs the card and brings back the slip. (3 mins)
5. Guest signs and leaves. (2 mins)

That is 12 minutes of dead time where the table isn't generating revenue. By allowing guests to pay directly from their phones using Apple Pay or Google Pay, you reclaim that 12 minutes instantly. Over a 5-hour shift, saving 12 minutes per table means seating an entire extra party at that table.

By removing the administrative bottlenecks of ordering and paying, you allow your staff to focus on hospitality, while maximizing your revenue potential.
